    About the music & credits

    New, piano-only accompaniments arranged for congregational singing. The notes are played with Alexander Holm’s Salamander Grand Piano, sampled from a Yamaha C5 (CC BY 3.0). The performances use pitched, layered samples with newly programmed phrasing and dynamics; they are not live takes.

    ASH GROVE: traditional Welsh melody; words by Katherine K. Davis. ITALIAN HYMN: Felice de Giardini, 1769; anonymous words. THANKFULNESS: Keith Getty and Stuart Townend, © 2003 Thankyou Music. OLD HUNDREDTH: Genevan Psalter, attributed to Louis Bourgeois; words by Thomas Ken. Historical scores: The Open Hymnal Project. Other score and lyric credits appear with the music.
